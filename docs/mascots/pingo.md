# Pingo

## Identity

- Id: `pingo`
- Theme: water elemental
- Personality: curious and adaptable
- Primary silhouette: rounded droplet with a pointed crown
- Primary accent: cyan

Pingo is the reference mascot for the desktop client's first vertical slice.

## Mandatory visual anchors

- droplet-shaped outer silhouette;
- pointed water crown;
- bright rectangular or rounded eyes;
- small wave-like mouth;
- low, rounded base;
- optional water droplets or splash marks in the effect margin.

At least the droplet silhouette and water crown must remain visible in every living stage.

## Stage direction

| Engine stage | Direction                                                        |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `egg`        | Smooth water core with no limbs and one small crown point        |
| `baby`       | First compact guide form; tiny body and minimal face             |
| `child`      | Second guide form; broader base and clearer arms                 |
| `teen`       | Taller drop, asymmetrical splash crest, more active posture      |
| `adult`      | Third guide form; developed water crest and surrounding droplets |
| `elder`      | Wide, calm form with layered wave base and restrained crest      |

The guide's three forms anchor `baby`, `child`, and `adult`. Egg, teen, and elder require production drawings that preserve the anchors above.

## Expression direction

The approved concept set includes happy, sad, hungry, sleeping, playing, sick, and evolving studies. Production must extend those expressions to dirty, medicine, discipline, attention, and dead states using the shared animation contract.

Pingo's water crown is the main emotional secondary channel:

- upright and bright when happy;
- lowered when sad or sick;
- gently curved when sleeping;
- split into droplets during play or evolution.
