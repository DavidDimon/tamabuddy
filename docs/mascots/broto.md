# Broto

## Identity

- Id: `broto`
- Theme: nature elemental
- Personality: calm and careful
- Primary silhouette: round seed body with a stem and leaves
- Primary accent: green

## Mandatory visual anchors

- centered stem emerging from the head;
- one or two readable leaves;
- round body with a flat or root-like base;
- bright eyes and small friendly mouth;
- foliage remains distinct from temporary dirt effects.

## Stage direction

| Engine stage | Direction                                              |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| `egg`        | Closed seed with a tiny seam                           |
| `baby`       | First guide form; single sprout and compact body       |
| `child`      | Second guide form; paired leaves and broader base      |
| `teen`       | Taller stem and budding side growth                    |
| `adult`      | Third guide form; small tree canopy and rooted feet    |
| `elder`      | Rounded old-tree form with a fuller but quieter canopy |

Broto evolves by adding plant structure while retaining the original round seed body.
