# Roadmap contract

A roadmap orders outcomes whose dependencies or learning affect later work. It is not a flat backlog and does not claim calendar precision without evidence.

Each milestone names:

- the outcome and why it comes now;
- entry conditions and dependencies;
- deliverables or capabilities, not an exhaustive speculative file list;
- an observable exit criterion or demonstration;
- risks, decisions, or measurements produced for later milestones.

Keep discovery, architectural decisions, implementation, migration, and enforcement distinguishable when they carry different risk. Prefer a vertical proof before broad migration. Mark optional work and stop conditions explicitly.
